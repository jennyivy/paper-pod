Agents-K1 aims to revolutionize how AI interprets scientific documents, making it a key advancement in AI-driven research. This paper introduces a new end-to-end system called Agents-K1 that transforms raw academic papers into detailed, multimodal knowledge graphs. The core idea is to move from simple agent orchestration to a sophisticated knowledge orchestration that captures the full depth of scientific reasoning.

The paper integrates three major components. First, a multimodal parser that parses not just abstracts, but entire papers, analyzing text, figures, tables, and equations as interconnected evidence. It then builds an enriched graph of scientific knowledge. Second, a lightweight yet powerful information extraction model powered by Group Relative Policy Optimization, by using a rule-based reward system. This model holds its own against others with twice as many parameters and consistently exceeds benchmarks. Third, the GraphAnything command line interface, which seamlessly combines web search, graph retrieval, and document traversal into a single platform for agents to execute complex research tasks.

Concrete results underline its capabilities. Agents-K1 processes over two million papers, spanning six scientific fields, to create Scholar-KG, a vast knowledge graph, with a million-paper subset available for broader research use. In practice, this system lifts Gemini-3 overall accuracy on the FrontierScience-Research benchmark from under eight percent to nearly twenty-five percent, and significantly boosts reasoning accuracy on complex multi-hop QA datasets like HotpotQA and MuSiQue.

A key limitation is the focus on scientific literature, which may not directly translate to broader domains without schema adjustments. However, the framework's adaptability suggests future expansion capabilities.

Researchers in AI and industries reliant on scientific literature should explore Agents-K1. It offers structured and comprehensive insights into complex academic content, an essential tool for scalable and context-aware AI research workflows.
