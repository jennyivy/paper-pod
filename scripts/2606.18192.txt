Introducing the Stanford EDGAR Filings Dataset, a transformative resource in the realm of financial language models, crafted to offer layout-faithful and token-efficient pretraining data for machine learning.

This paper tackles the growing scarcity of clean, long-context documents for large language models. Its primary innovation is turning SEC filings into a meticulous MultiMarkdown format, enhancing their suitability for financial reasoning and document understanding. By doing so, it opens up an abundant yet previously underutilized resource for those developing financial models.

Now, let's hit the highlights. The Stanford EDGAR Filings Dataset, abbreviated as SEFD, is a treasure trove of around 152 billion tokens. It captures key documents like financial statements and risk disclosures, parsed from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's vast repository. SEFD's conversion methods shine in maintaining critical document layouts, which are crucial for financial reasoning. This dataset boasts a staggering structural and semantic accuracy above 99 percent, ensuring that models can grasp the complexity of financial tables and document hierarchies. Remarkably, SEFD contains less than 0.1 percent overlap with commonly used public web corpora, providing an exceptionally unique resource.

The authors additionally introduce two benchmarks—EDGAR-Forecast and EDGAR-OCR. EDGAR-Forecast is designed to evaluate a model's prowess in forecasting company financials beyond its training data cutoff, with GPT-5.5 reaching a score of 51.8 percent across the benchmark tasks. Meanwhile, EDGAR-OCR evaluates how well models can transcribe intricate financial tables into HTML, showing the dataset's power to essentially improve text-table processing, with top models scoring nearly 76 percent.

However, one limitation worth noting is that SEFD relies on the quality of Optical Character Recognition for PDFs, which could affect the accuracy of PDF-derived content. Parser coverage might also struggle with new or altered SEC filing schemas.

What's the bottom line here? SEFD is a groundbreaking tool for anyone involved in financial data modeling or language processing. It empowers you to dive into a wealth of high-quality, sophisticated financial documents with minimal overlap with other datasets, making it a vital resource for developing cutting-edge financial language models.
