"Benchmark Everything Everywhere All at Once" tackles a significant challenge in machine learning: the construction and evolution of benchmarks necessary to evaluate machine learning models, especially large language models and multimodal models. The core contribution of the paper is the introduction of Benchmark Agent, an autonomous system that automates the creation and adaptation of benchmarks, addressing the sustainability and scalability issues of current human-driven methods. This system effectively automates the entire pipeline, from user query analysis to data annotation and quality control, drastically reducing the reliance on human effort and enabling rapid iteration and adaptation of benchmarks.

Here are the key highlights. Benchmark Agent is evaluated across fifteen representative benchmarks, covering areas like text understanding, multimodal comprehension, and domain-specific reasoning. It shows that current models still struggle with some domain-specific tasks, indicating areas for further research. Importantly, it demonstrates the ability to produce high-quality benchmarks with minimal human involvement, maintaining reliable quality that rivals traditional human-constructed benchmarks. The agent-framework features a dual-component design: a Benchmark Planner and a Benchmark Executor, enabling efficient and flexible customization of benchmarks according to specific user needs. This allows for rapid updates in response to new models and evolving domains.

One significant limitation is that while Benchmark Agent reduces human labor, expert review can still enhance outcomes. More interactive feedback mechanisms could improve precision without substantial additional cost.

The paper is essential reading for researchers and practitioners needing efficient and adaptable benchmark solutions. It offers a robust platform for developing and evaluating models continuously, crucial in an era of rapid advancements in large language and multimodal models.
